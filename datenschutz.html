Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the game “Varnek” for Android (the “App”) and to the website varnekrpg.github.io. This English version is a translation. In case of differences, the German version prevails. Who is responsible for the processing and how to reach us is stated at the end of this page.

In short

  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We have no access to them.
  • The App itself does not send any data to us or to third parties.

1. Data that stays on your device

Save games and settings are stored only on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

The App does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

The App does not send any data to us in the process. Google’s Privacy Policy applies to Google Play, and the app you share with has its own privacy policy.
